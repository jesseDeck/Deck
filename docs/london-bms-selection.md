# London broker management system targets

For this implementation, the top integration targets were set to:

1. **Acturis**
2. **Open GI**
3. **SSP**

## Why these three

These are repeatedly referenced in UK broker software market coverage as primary broker platform providers and are commonly treated as core systems in London/UK commercial and personal lines broker operations.

Examples reviewed:

- Insurance Times eTrading coverage identifying Acturis, Open GI, and SSP among leading software houses.
- Insurance Business UK reporting that highlights Acturis as market leader with Open GI and SSP as principal providers.

> Note: this repository uses configurable source URLs so you can point each system at your actual London-market tenant login endpoint rather than a public marketing domain.
